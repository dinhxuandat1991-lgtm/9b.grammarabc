DIANA’s online post

Hello to all my friends. I love it here in Venice! It’s so beautiful – even in the winter. I have to spend a lot of time on my art history classes, but at the weekends I get some free time. I usually walk around and look at the old buildings, or when it’s cold, I go to museums. There are so many interesting little shops here too – it’s great. It’s very different from going to a shopping centre. This week it’s Carnevale and the whole city is like one big party. Yesterday my friends invited me to a big party in a piazza (that’s Italian for a town square). It’s tonight and I have to wear a long dress and mask. I need to go shopping!
